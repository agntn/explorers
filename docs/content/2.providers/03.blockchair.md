## Address it

```ts
const blockchair = await create("blockchair"); // BLOCKCHAIR_API_KEY is optional
await blockchair.getBalance("1A1zP1eP5QGefi2DMPTfTL5SLmv7DivfNa", "bitcoin");
```

The key is optional. Set `BLOCKCHAIR_API_KEY` and Blockchair joins the configured tier of [provider selection](/guide/selection); leave it unset and it stays a keyless candidate that shows up in the fallback pass, behind the providers that need no key, so after Mempool and Blockstream on Bitcoin and after Mempool on Litecoin.

## What it reads

`/{chain}/dashboards/address/:address` for balance and history, `/{chain}/dashboards/transaction/:hash` for one transaction, `/{chain}/dashboards/block/:number` for a block. Balances, history, details and blocks; no contracts, tokens, gas or unspent outputs.

## Gotchas

- The data format differs between the UTXO chains and Ethereum, and the provider maps both. eCash amounts are satoshis at two decimals, so 100 satoshis is 1 XEC. Yes, two.
- Bitcoin Cash sits under `bitcoin-cash` in the URL, not the `bitcoincash` key. Its address dashboard takes CashAddr with or without the `bitcoincash:` prefix, and the old `1...` form too.
- Litecoin amounts are litoshis at eight decimals, under the plain `litecoin` slug. Without a key it's the second stop for Litecoin. When litecoinspace.org is down, a balance, history, transaction or block read lands here. Unspent outputs and fees have nowhere else to go.
- Zcash amounts are zatoshis at eight decimals, and they count the transparent side only. A shielded address has no public balance or history, and Blockchair reports a fully shielded transaction with value `0` and fee `0`, so a zero fee there tells you nothing.
- The dashboard answer carries `context.state`, the chain height when it was built, and that's what `Balance.blockNumber` shows. Every other provider's balance endpoint says nothing about a block.
- `funded` and `spent` are the address's cumulative received and spent totals, in satoshis.
- Blockchair has its own status codes for "too much": 402 and 435 to 437 for a spent limit, 430 and 434 when it has blocked your IP outright. The provider reads all of them as a `RateLimitError` carrying Blockchair's own reason, so an automatic read moves on to the next backend. Without a key, a blocked IP also gets told that `BLOCKCHAIR_API_KEY` lifts the block.
